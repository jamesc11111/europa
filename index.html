Europa Society at UCC

About the society

UCC Europa Society is a student-run organisation dedicated to raising awareness of the European Union and increasing engagement with it amongst students in University College Cork.

We run various information events and social media campaigns on topics such as how the EU works, key EU policies, Erasmus+ opportunities, and careers within the EU institutions. We regularly host guest speakers including MEPs, academics, translators and ambassadors, who share their experiences with students. We have collaborated with partners including EPLO Ireland, European Movement Ireland and other student societies both within UCC and elsewhere in Ireland.

In 2024, we organised a trip for our members to visit the European institutions in Brussels. In 2025, we were selected to facilitate a workshop at the European Youth Event in the European Parliament in Strasbourg, themed around defending democracy and the rule of law.

The society also promotes and celebrates European cultures on campus, building a sense of community and championing the EU motto “United in Diversity”. Our events include music nights, intercultural coffee mornings, and our annual Culture and Community Week and Cultural Party held in collaboration with UCC’s cultural societies.

We’d be delighted to welcome any UCC student as a member of our society! You can learn about our upcoming events by following us on social media at @ucceuropasoc.

The County Pairings Project

The inspiration behind the project

The inspiration behind the County Pairings Project stems from the EU Presidency County Pairings initiative by Ministers Helen McEntee and Thomas Byrne in January 2026. From initially seeing the proposal on a LinkedIn post, I placed a great value on the unique initiative. I must give credit to the Ministers on creating an initiative to connect local populations with the EU. One of the past committee members of University College Cork Europa Society, Leah Houdet created a quiz based on the initiative, researching the links between EU countries and Irish counties. For example, Cork is paired with France for the EU Presidency and the French Huguenots came over to Cork in the late 17th century. This link sparked my curiosity to develop this project.

Furthermore, I feel the County Pairing initiative can go further. After asking students what EU country their home county is associated it, they hadn’t heard of the pairings. The aim of this project is to shed light on the government’s initiative, providing information of EU countries and their respective Irish county pairing. Individuals also have the chance to contribute on how the EU has impacted their own lives.

While I came up with this idea in early July, it has taken time to go from thoughts on a page to the website you are now reading. This project would not have been possible with some incredible people, which I am forever grateful for. Initially, I mentioned the idea to James Coakley who 2 hours later sent me back a link to the website that I had envisioned in my head. I appreciate the time you have taken to develop this website and turn my sometimes crazy visions into reality. Furthermore, I want to extend my sincere gratitude to Susana Gomez, Europa’s Chairperson who was unwavering in her support and committed to seeing this project come to fruition. Additionally, to Josh Hanover, UCC Societies President whom answered my long list of questions regarding this project. I also couldn’t have done it without the UCC Europa Society committee who helped populate the website with research and imagery. Thank you all so much.

So, what is my contribution?

The EU has impacted my life through my involvement in the UCC Europa Society over the past two years. The friends I’ve met and the memories I’ve made, over the common bond of the EU will be cherished for a lifetime.

I ask you to now make yours.

Go raibh maith agat,

Claire Foyle
